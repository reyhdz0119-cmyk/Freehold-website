Questions people ask first

Does it sync across my phone and computer?

Not automatically — it's intentionally local-only, which is the whole privacy pitch. You can export a backup file and import it on another device manually whenever you want to move your data.

What if I lose my computer or clear my browser data?

As long as you've used Export Backup, you can restore everything instantly from that file. That backup habit is the one thing worth building — everything else is automatic.

Is my passcode actually secure?

It's a screen lock — enough to stop someone from casually opening the app and seeing your numbers. It isn't encryption, and we'd rather tell you that plainly than let you assume otherwise.

Can I connect my bank account?

No, and that's by design. Everything is entered manually, which is also exactly why nothing about your finances ever touches a server.

Do I need to know Excel or spreadsheets?

No — it's a real app interface with buttons, tabs, and dropdowns, not a spreadsheet. If you've used any budgeting app before, this will feel familiar.
