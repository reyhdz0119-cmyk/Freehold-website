Download for Windows

This link always points to the newest release. Windows will likely warn that the installer is from an "unidentified publisher" — that's expected for an app this size without a paid code-signing certificate; it doesn't mean anything is wrong with the file.

First time installing?

Run the installer, open Freehold, and it'll show you a machine code on screen. Copy that code, then go to the activation page and enter it along with the license key from your Gumroad receipt. You'll get an activation key instantly — paste it into Freehold and you're done, on this one computer, with no account and no internet connection needed after that.

Already have Freehold installed?

Freehold never connects to the internet, so it can't check for updates by itself. Instead, about every 30 days it reminds you to visit this page and see if there's a newer version. You can also choose "Look for Updates" from the Freehold menu at any time. Installing a new version over your current one keeps your data and your activation.

Lost your license key?

Your license key is in the Gumroad receipt email from your purchase, and in your Gumroad library. If you can't find it, email support@getfreehold.net with your purchase details.
